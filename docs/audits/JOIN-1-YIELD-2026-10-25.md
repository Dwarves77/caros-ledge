## No word of the prose is matched (entity reach)

Operator and scope reviewer, 2026-10-25: the flywheel never filters free text with word lists or phrase regexes. `src/core/coupling/resolve.ts` now reads two formal codes and nothing else: an instrument identifier ("Regulation (EU) YYYY/NNNN", CELEX), by equality through the parse module, and a UN/LOCODE (two letters and three letters or digits, whole upper-case token), by equality against the identifiers the spine holds. `refs.ts` no longer reads entity names, aliases, instrument titles or acronyms, and a couplings.json `alias` map is inert (the typed-column pass honours `entity`, `mode`, `map`, `const` and `refs`). A jurisdiction attaches only from a typed column; a publisher's host stays on its source. The alias-seed and acronym seeding of the seeds pass is removed with the files that fed it.
